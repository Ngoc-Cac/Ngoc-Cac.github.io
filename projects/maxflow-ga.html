Rerouting Airline Passengers of Canceled Flights using Genetic Algorithms

This is one of my first AI project, where we investigated the use of Genetic Algorithms to reroute airline passengers such that they can still complete their journey in the case where their flight is canceled.

Tech Stack: Python, Tkinter, PyInstaller

This project started out as a coursework, where we tried to implement search algorithms that were covered throughout the course. For our group, it was to design a genetic algorithm to solve the maximum flow problem.

As the initial outcome, we developed a genetic algorithm that operates on weight matrices, representing the target network. Encouraged by our professor to publish our results, we decided to apply our algorithms to air traffic flow management problems, modeling flight routes between cities as a flow network.

With a passenger-centered objective, we investigated whether one could determine alternative routes for passengers whose flights had been canceled, helping them complete their journeys with minimal disruption. Using a crossover scheme that prioritises flow conservation, we obtained fast rerouting for graphs with around 10-30 vertices, with the final flow values deviating by 1-2 passengers from the global maximum.

Personally, this was an eye-opening project for me. Although we didn't investigate any modern tools or frameworks, this was my first exposure to research. Through the project, I learnt how to formalise a problem, being able to investigate and review relevant literature and turn ideas into the working implementation.
